This is the first post. I built this site to have a home for the things I make and the things I learn along the way.

## What you'll find here

I work across hardware and software, so expect a mix:

- **Digital design** — notes from RTL, verification, and the occasional FPGA detour.
- **Software** — small tools, automation, and web experiments.
- **Process** — how I learn, ship, and explain technical things.

## Why write at all

Writing forces clarity. If I can't explain a thing simply, I probably don't understand it yet. So this blog is partly for you and partly for me.

> The best way to learn is to build, then explain what you built.
